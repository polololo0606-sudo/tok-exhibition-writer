# TOK exhibition writer

A portable instruction file for developing IB Theory of Knowledge exhibition drafts from a student's own selected objects, images and confirmed context.

**[Download or read SKILL.md](SKILL.md)**

## Use

1. Download `SKILL.md` and attach it to an AI conversation, or paste its contents. Explicitly ask the assistant to follow the skill. The host must support reading the supplied instructions and images.
2. Provide your selected official exhibition prompt, or your list of allowed prompts.
3. Supply three objects you have already chosen, with a photograph, screenshot or attributed image of each specific object.
4. Answer the assistant's focused questions about context and evidence, then confirm or correct the proposed interpretations.
5. Review the draft, references and remaining limitations. Verify the claims, understand the reasoning and follow your school's rules for acknowledging AI assistance.

The skill can be supplied as instructions to Codex, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Attaching it does not install tools, grant browsing permissions or enable image recognition.

## What it does

- Keeps object selection with the student. It never proposes objects or replacements.
- Develops object-specific analysis, explained prompt links and justified contributions using confirmed facts and appropriate evidence.
- Targets the qualities of the 9-10/10 exhibition band, with 900-930 commentary words as a working target and a 950-word ceiling.
- Uses British English by default, avoids em dashes in its own prose and maintains citations and image credits.
- Includes a bounded ZeroGPT workflow when authorised and available. It checks a citation-free copy, preserves the referenced master, rejects revisions that damage the argument and records results against exact text versions.

The requested ZeroGPT threshold is below 15%. That is a workflow preference, not an IB criterion or proof of authorship. A score cannot guarantee a grade, establish that writing is human-authored or remove acknowledgment obligations. If the site cannot be operated, the assistant can prepare text for a manual check and identify results as student-reported.

## Research and validation

The source register is embedded in `SKILL.md`. It records assessment guidance and lessons from 17 reviewed exhibitions, distinguishing inspected examiner evidence from school or host-reported marks. It also documents coverage limits. Source PDFs and other students' complete exhibitions are not redistributed in this repository.

The [strict review](REVIEW.md) documents 24 manual stress cases and a citation-removal fixture. Structural validation passed. Those checks were not a live ZeroGPT test, an independent examiner assessment or an end-to-end benchmark across AI platforms.

No grade or detector result is guaranteed. An A is an overall TOK grade; the exhibition component is marked out of 10. This is an independent resource, not an official IB publication.
